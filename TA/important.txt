INDICATORS TO AVOID (AND WHY)

This part is extremely important and rarely explained well.

❌ Indicators That Are Often Misused
1️⃣ Too Many Oscillators Together

Examples:

    RSI + Stochastic + Williams %R

    Why to avoid

    They measure the SAME thing (momentum)

    Highly correlated → fake confirmation

    ✅ Fix: Use ONE oscillator + ONE trend indicator

2️⃣ Lag-on-Lag Indicators

Examples:

    SMA + EMA + MACD together

    Why to avoid

    All are lagging

    You react late to reversals

    ✅ Fix: Combine lagging (EMA) + leading (RSI / OBV)

3️⃣ Indicators Without Context

Example:

    RSI overbought in strong trend

    Why wrong

    RSI can stay >70 for long time in trends

    Causes premature exits

    ✅ Fix: Use RSI ONLY when ADX < 20 (range)

4️⃣ Ichimoku + Multiple MAs

    Why to avoid

    Ichimoku already includes averages

    Redundant & cluttered charts

    ✅ Fix: Ichimoku alone OR EMA-based system

5️⃣ Volume Indicators on Low-Quality Data

Examples:

    OBV / VWAP on illiquid assets

    Why to avoid

    Garbage volume = garbage signals

    ✅ Fix: Use volume indicators only on liquid markets

🧠 Professional Rule of Thumb

    Never use more than one indicator per category

    Category	        Pick ONE
    Trend	            EMA or Ichimoku
    Momentum	        RSI
    Volatility	        ATR
    Volume	            VWAP or OBV
